# 99Pluz Blog Audit

**Website audit of 99pluz.com/blog**
Checked 19 and 20 November 2026 · About 60 pages reviewed · Platform: Wix

The blog works and every link loads. The problems are in trust, findability, speed, polish and design consistency. Most fixes take minutes in the Wix editor, and none need a developer.

## The four things to fix first

1. **Delete the staff writing notes** in the Sports, Politics and Tech category descriptions, which Google shows in search results.
2. **Label paid client posts**, as the About page already promises.
3. **Link to sources** in news and fact-check articles.
4. **Remove or hide the 14 "copy-of" draft pages** that Google can currently find.

| Severity | Count |
|---|---|
| High | 5 |
| Medium | 7 |
| Low | 5 |

## Area 1: Trust and credibility

These issues affect how readers, clients and Google judge whether the blog is a reliable source. They matter most because 99Pluz sells PR on the strength of its editorial reputation.

### 🔴 High: Internal writing notes show up in Google results

The Sports category description reads like a brief for writers. It isn't shown on the page itself, but it sits in the page code and is what Google shows in search results:

> Tone: energetic but clear; celebrate performance, examine systems… Content types: match recaps, nominee lists, player milestones, short explainers. Goal: drive fandom and context.

The Politics description also opens with tone notes for writers. The Tech page ends with "Curious, forward-looking, and grounded in real human impact." Anyone who finds these pages through Google reads these notes first.

**Fix:** In Wix Blog, open Categories and rewrite each description for readers. Move the writing guidance into an internal style guide.

### 🔴 High: Client posts are not labelled as paid or sponsored

The About page says: "Paid campaigns and sponsored content are clearly labelled." Three of the four newest posts feature one artist, Great Adamz. Another covers a listening party hosted by the 99Pluz publicist. None of them carries a sponsored, client or partnership label.

**Fix:** Add a short line at the top of every client post, such as "This is a 99Pluz client." Use a "Partner" tag so these posts can be filtered. In the UK, the Advertising Standards Authority (ASA) expects paid content to be clearly marked.

### 🔴 High: News and fact-check articles link to no sources

The article "The UK Isn't Telling You To Stockpile Because Russia Is Coming" refers to a July government announcement, the National Risk Register, a Government Office for Science assessment from 17 September, and a Bundeswehr exercise. It links to none of them. The only outbound links on the page are the site's own social accounts.

**Fix:** Link each claim to its original source. Add a "Sources" list at the end of every news, politics and fact-check post.

### 🟠 Medium: Every post has the same author

The author profile lists 299 posts, all by Chinenye Mbakwe, Head of Operations. The site presents itself as a fact-checked editorial platform, and one name on every story looks thin for that claim.

**Fix:** Credit contributors and editors where they exist. Write a fuller author bio. Add a short "Editorial standards" page and link it from every post.

### 🟠 Medium: The blog has no clear focus

The business is music PR and brand strategy. The blog also covers UK defence planning, semiconductors, billionaire space investment and Nigerian football debates. That spreads the site's authority across topics it isn't known for.

**Fix:** Decide which topics the blog owns. Keep culture pieces that link back to music and entertainment. Consider dropping or merging Politics, Tech and Sports.

## Area 2: Search and site structure

These issues affect how easily Google and readers find content on the site.

### 🔴 High: Unfinished duplicate pages can be found by search engines

The site's sitemap, which tells Google which pages to index, lists 14 extra pages. Their addresses don't match what they show, and five have no description.

| Address | What the page actually shows |
|---|---|
| `copy-of-press-media-features` | Editorial Playlist Recommendations |
| `copy-of-press-media-features-1` | Brand (Press & Media Page) |
| `copy-of-press-media-features-2` | Chart Placements and Visibility |
| `copy-of-events-coverage` | Content Coverage |
| `copy-of-events-coverage-1` | Brand (Events Coverage) |
| `copy-of-events-coverage-2` | Brand Rebranding & Identity Development |
| `copy-of-brand-events-coverage` | Ads Content Creation |
| `copy-of-ads-content-creation` | Bespoke Campain of Ads Content Creation |
| `copy-of-brand-press-media-page` | Social Media Campaigns |
| `copy-of-editorial-playlist-recommenda` | Events Coverage |
| `copy-of-blog-writer-page` | Social Media Growth Page |
| `s-projects-basic-1` | Press & Media Features |
| `about-4` | Blog Writer Page |
| `inquiry-services-page` | Inquiry Services Page |

**Fix:** Delete pages that are drafts or duplicates. For pages you keep, rename the address in Wix page SEO settings, and Wix will offer to set up a redirect from the old one. Hide any page that shouldn't appear in search.

### 🟠 Medium: Main pages have default Wix addresses

Six core pages kept the address Wix gave them when they were created.

| Page | Current address | Suggested |
|---|---|---|
| Privacy Policy | `/s-projects-side-by-side` | `/privacy-policy` |
| Careers | `/coming-soon-01` | `/careers` |
| About | `/about-3` | `/about` |
| Brand & Creative Strategy | `/copy-of-services` | `/brand-strategy` |
| Music PR | `/services-2` | `/music-pr` |
| General Enquiries | `/contact-7` | `/contact` |

**Fix:** Rename each address and accept Wix's offer to redirect the old one, so existing links keep working.

### 🟠 Medium: The newest posts have no search description

The four most recent posts have no meta description, the short summary Google shows under a search result. Older posts do have one, so the step was dropped recently. Without it, Google writes its own summary.

**Fix:** Add a description in each post's SEO settings before publishing. Make it part of the publishing checklist.

### 🟠 Medium: Page headings are set up wrongly

The homepage has no main heading. The About page has seven main headings where it should have one. The blog's main heading is just "All Posts," which tells Google nothing about the blog.

**Fix:** Give each page one main heading that says what it is. On About, change the six section titles to the second heading level. On the blog, use something like "Afrobeats news, culture and music PR insights."

### 🟠 Medium: The blog shows only five posts per page

With 299 posts, the archive runs to about 60 pages. Older work is hard for readers to reach.

**Fix:** Show 12 to 20 posts per page. Add "Popular" or "Start here" collections, and link related posts inside articles.

### 🟠 Medium: Four of the 19 categories are empty

Events, Playlists, Resources and Brand Spotlights show "Posts Coming Soon," but all four are in the menu. Events is listed first, so many readers land on an empty page.

**Fix:** Hide empty categories until they have at least three posts. Put the strongest categories first.

### 🟢 Low: Labels and descriptions don't match their pages

The menu says "General Enquiries," but the page heading says "General Services." The About page's search description starts "Get in touch," copied from Contact. The homepage description stops mid-thought at "the intersection of music." Careers, the Privacy Policy, Search and the author profile have no description.

**Fix:** Write one accurate description for every page, and make menu labels match page headings.

## Area 3: Speed and reliability

### 🔴 High: Article pages load slowly, and one request failed

Measured from a single connection on 19 November 2026. Times vary with location and network, so treat these as indicative.

| Measure | Result |
|---|---|
| Blog page code size | 1.25 MB |
| Full load time of article pages | 4.5 to 17.7 s |
| Slowest wait for the server to respond | 10.9 s |
| Requests that timed out completely | 1 |

Most of the page weight is Wix's own code, which the team can't change directly. What the team controls is how much is on each page.

**Fix:** Run the Wix Site Speed dashboard and Google PageSpeed Insights on a few articles. Remove unused apps and widgets, such as the store and AI assistant code that loads on blog pages. Compress large PNG images before upload, and use JPEG or WebP for photos.

## Area 4: Accessibility and polish

### 🟢 Low: Images are missing descriptions

Each article has one or two images with no alt text, which is what screen readers read aloud. The logo's alt text is a filename, "top music PR company_2523.PNG."

**Fix:** Add a short description to every image in a post. Change the logo's alt text to "99Pluz."

### 🟢 Low: Typos appear on service pages

"Campain" appears in a page name that loads on every page. The Music PR page says "playlist lacements" instead of "placements."

**Fix:** Correct both, and spell-check service pages before they go live.

### 🟢 Low: Some category descriptions read oddly

The How-To description opens with "How To is where clarity lives." Lifestyle says it covers "everyday Nigerian life," while much of the recent coverage is about the UK.

**Fix:** Rewrite category descriptions in plain words that say what readers will find.

### 🟢 Low: The Careers page looks unfinished

It sits at a "coming-soon" address, has no description, and lists two roles with only an Apply button and no details.

**Fix:** Add a short summary for each role and the closing date, and rename the address.

## Area 5: UI and UX critique

Reviewed on 20 November 2026 from screenshots of 14 pages at desktop size and five at phone size: Home, Blog, a category with posts, an empty category, an article, About, Music PR, Brand & Creative Strategy, a service detail page, Contact, Careers, Privacy Policy, Search and the author profile. Wix serves phones a separate fixed-width layout, so phone findings are close but not exact.

Each item names the design area it belongs to: hierarchy, brand, composition, typography, colour, affordance (how clearly things look clickable) or density (how much is on screen at once).

### P1: Critical, fix first

- [ ] **A pop-up blocks every page on arrival.** The "Get Monthly Updates" pop-up covers the whole screen before any content, and fills the entire display on phones. Seen on Home, Blog, articles, Contact and Brand. *Affordance.* **Fix:** show it after half the page is scrolled, once per visitor, as a small bar on phones.
- [ ] **Orange text on white fails contrast.** This affects About headings, service headings, orange italic "Deliverables" text and form labels. White text on orange buttons such as "View Details", "Explore" and "Start Now" fails too. *Colour.* **Fix:** keep brand orange for fills and dark backgrounds. On white, use a darker orange of about #b85a12 for text, and put dark text on orange buttons, as "Get In Touch" already does. #b85a12 only just passes, so don't go any lighter.
- [ ] **The category bar on article pages is unreadable.** It is grey text on orange, on desktop and phone. *Colour.* **Fix:** use a black bar with white text and an orange marker for the active category.
- [ ] **The testimonial slider is broken on Contact and Brand.** A stray blue bar sits above it, a pink arrow floats out of place, and the left arrow runs through the person's name. *Composition.* **Fix:** replace it with simple quote cards showing a photo, name and role.
- [ ] **About text breaks mid-sentence.** The copy was pasted with hard line breaks, so lines end at "brands", "not" and "coverage". It looks broken on phones. *Typography.* **Fix:** re-paste it as plain paragraphs.
- [ ] **Service headings spill out of their frames.** On Brand & Creative Strategy and Editorial Playlist Placements, the title runs outside its outlined pill over a busy photo. *Typography.* **Fix:** remove the pill and put the title on a solid or darkened background.

| Colour pair | Contrast | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Brand orange #ec8835 on white | 2.6 : 1 | Fails. Body text needs 4.5, large text needs 3 |
| White on brand orange | 2.6 : 1 | Fails |
| Brand orange on black #0d0d0d | 7.5 : 1 | Passes |
| Darker orange #b85a12 on white | 4.7 : 1 | Passes |

### P2: Important

- [ ] **Pages switch between light and dark.** The blog list is black, articles are white, About is all white, and Home and Music PR alternate bands. *Brand.* **Fix:** make black the base, since orange works best there, and use white only for long reading areas such as article text.
- [ ] **Too many typefaces.** League Spartan, Arial, Poppins, Avenir, Helvetica and the Wix default font all appear, and one team card uses four styles. *Typography.* **Fix:** choose one heading font and one body font, with a scale of five or six sizes. This is where the planned font change goes.
- [ ] **The homepage hero doesn't say what 99Pluz does.** The biggest words are "Brand Buzz", one slide of a slideshow. The real tagline is a small line above it, and "Get In Touch" floats far right. The tagline repeats in the next section, and a scrolling ticker repeats "The 99Pluz". *Hierarchy.* **Fix:** write one headline that says who 99Pluz helps and how, add two buttons such as "Read the blog" and "Work with us", and drop the slideshow and ticker.
- [ ] **Blog thumbnails are reused Instagram graphics.** They have text baked in, one says "SWIPE TO READ>>>>>>" on a page that can't be swiped, and their uneven heights make a ragged grid. *Brand.* **Fix:** use clean photos cropped to one shape, with the headline as real text.
- [ ] **Each place shows posts differently.** All Posts uses a staggered grid, while categories, the homepage and search each use their own list style. *Composition.* **Fix:** use one card design everywhere.
- [ ] **Small view counts hurt credibility.** Cards show counts like "3 views" and "0 comments". *Brand.* **Fix:** turn off counts in Wix Blog settings.
- [ ] **Articles are hard to read.** Lines run about 95 characters wide, with no space between paragraphs, so the text becomes a wall. *Typography.* **Fix:** limit lines to about 70 characters, add space between paragraphs, and use 18px body text.
- [ ] **On phones, articles start too low.** The header plus two bars take up about a third of the first screen, and the AI chat bubble covers the lead image. *Density.* **Fix:** move categories into the menu, and remove or shrink the chat bubble on articles.
- [ ] **Service grids are misaligned.** Buttons sit at different heights and fifth cards are left on their own. Icons come from different sets, including a hand-drawn champagne glass and a hotel bell for chart placements. *Composition.* **Fix:** use equal-height cards with buttons pinned to the bottom and one icon set.
- [ ] **The Contact page buries its form.** The heading says "General Services" while the menu says Enquiries. A green Scrabble stock photo clashes with the brand, and a small form sits below two testimonials. *Hierarchy.* **Fix:** put the form and the email and phone details first. The label mismatch is also covered in Area 2.
- [ ] **The consultation forms are cluttered.** Social icons sit between the fields, labels are small orange text, required fields aren't marked, and "Campaings" is misspelt. *Affordance.* **Fix:** remove the icons, use dark labels at 14px or larger, and mark required fields.
- [ ] **The Careers page looks unbranded.** Its headline is literally "Careers Page". Job cards use pink-purple and yellow-green gradients, half the row is empty, and roles have no details. *Brand.* **Fix:** use brand colours, add a role summary, and give each listing its own page. See also Area 4.
- [ ] **Empty categories have a large banner.** Events shows a big photo banner, then "Posts Coming Soon". *Density.* **Fix:** hide categories until they have posts, as in Area 2.
- [ ] **The footer is thin.** The logo is clipped at the top, there are only two links, and the phone footer has no contact details or links. *Composition.* **Fix:** add menu links, contact details and the Privacy Policy to every footer.

### P3: Polish

- [ ] **Category banners waste space.** They leave a big empty black area under the title, and card excerpts are cut off mid-line. *Composition.*
- [ ] **The Privacy Policy uses random stock photos,** such as an unrelated chart and a retro TV. *Brand.* Remove them.
- [ ] **"Rhythms" is misspelt two ways** on the playlist page, as "Ryhtms" and "Rythms". *Typography.*
- [ ] **The author avatar is a purple "N".** Purple isn't a brand colour. *Brand.* Use a real photo.
- [ ] **"Log In" is low-contrast grey** and leads to a member area readers don't need. *Affordance.* Remove it.
- [ ] **The team cards don't line up.** The photo is centred but the text is left-aligned, and one person is listed by first name only. *Composition.*
- [ ] **The homepage About collage is rough.** Images are cropped at the edges, and it includes a Spotify screenshot and a social-icons graphic as photos. *Brand.*
- [ ] **The newsletter sign-up appears twice on the homepage,** in the pop-up and again in a section with a generic stock photo of records. *Density.*

### Overall assessment

The strongest point is the core brand pairing. Orange on black is bold, readable and right for Afrobeats culture, and the black header with the 99+ logo works. The weakest point is consistency. Each page looks built from a different template, with its own fonts, backgrounds, icons, buttons and cards, so the site feels assembled rather than designed. A rebuild with one dark base, two fonts, one card design and one button style would fix most of the P2 list at once. It also fits the plan to keep the colours and change the fonts.
